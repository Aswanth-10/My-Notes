## 📌 Quick Tips Before You Begin

> [!tip] Email Writing Dos & Don'ts
> - ✅ Use **formal, polite** language
> - ✅ Keep subject line **clear and specific**
> - ✅ Start with a proper **salutation**
> - ✅ One clear **purpose per paragraph**
> - ✅ End with a **professional closing**
> - ❌ Avoid slang, casual language, or short forms
> - ❌ Don't skip the subject line
> - ❌ Avoid very long, rambling paragraphs

### Q1 – Write an email to your manager requesting a day off

**Scenario:** You need to take a day off due to a personal matter. Write an email to your manager seeking approval.

---

> [!example] Model Answer
>
> **To:** manager@company.com
> **Subject:** Request for a Day Off on [Date]
>
> Dear Mr./Ms. [Last Name],
>
> I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to request a day off on **[specific date]** due to a personal matter that requires my immediate attention.
>
> I assure you that I will complete all pending tasks before my leave and will coordinate with my teammates to ensure there is no disruption to ongoing work. I am also available on email should any urgent matter arise.
>
> I kindly request your approval at your earliest convenience. Please let me know if you need any additional information.
>
> Thank you for your understanding.
>
> Warm regards,
> [Your Name]
> [Designation] | [Department]

### Q2 – Write an email to a client apologizing for a delayed delivery

**Scenario:** Your team failed to deliver a project/product on time. Write an email to the client apologizing and giving an updated timeline.

---

> [!example] Model Answer
>
> **To:** client@clientcompany.com
> **Subject:** Apology for Delay in Delivery – [Project/Order Name]
>
> Dear Mr./Ms. [Client's Last Name],
>
> I sincerely apologize for the delay in delivering **[project/product name]**, which was originally scheduled for **[original date]**. We deeply regret any inconvenience this may have caused to your operations.
>
> The delay was primarily due to **[brief reason – e.g., an unexpected technical issue / supply chain disruption]**. We have taken immediate corrective measures and are confident in delivering the same by **[new delivery date]**.
>
> We value your trust and partnership greatly, and we are committed to ensuring this does not recur. Please feel free to reach out to me directly if you have any questions or concerns.
>
> Once again, we sincerely apologize for the inconvenience caused.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> [Your Name]
> [Designation] | [Company Name]
> [Contact Number]

### Q3 – Write an email to HR requesting a work-from-home arrangement

**Scenario:** Due to health or personal reasons, you want to request a temporary WFH arrangement from your HR department.

---

> [!example] Model Answer
>
> **To:** hr@company.com
> **Subject:** Request for Temporary Work-From-Home Arrangement
>
> Dear HR Team,
>
> I am writing to request a temporary work-from-home arrangement from **[start date]** to **[end date]** due to **[brief reason – e.g., a health concern / family emergency]**.
>
> I would like to assure you that my productivity and availability will remain unaffected during this period. I will attend all meetings virtually, meet deadlines as usual, and stay in constant communication with my team and manager.
>
> I have already discussed this with my reporting manager, **[Manager's Name]**, who has expressed support for this arrangement.
>
> I would be grateful if you could kindly consider and approve this request. Please let me know if any documentation or formality is required.
>
> Thank you for your time and consideration.
>
> Regards,
> [Your Name]
> [Employee ID] | [Department]

### Q4 – Write an email to a colleague requesting assistance on a project

**Scenario:** You are working on a tight deadline and need a colleague's expertise. Write a professional email requesting their help.

---

> [!example] Model Answer
>
> **To:** colleague@company.com
> **Subject:** Request for Assistance – [Project Name]
>
> Dear [Colleague's First Name],
>
> I hope you are doing well. I am currently working on **[project name]** and am facing a challenge in the area of **[specific topic/task]**. Given your expertise in this domain, I was hoping you could spare some time to guide me.
>
> If possible, could we schedule a brief meeting at your convenience — perhaps **[suggest a time/day]**? Alternatively, if you prefer, I can share the relevant documents and we can discuss over email.
>
> I understand you have your own commitments and I truly appreciate any help you can offer.
>
> Thank you in advance!
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]
> [Designation] | [Department]

### Q5 – Write an email to your team announcing a meeting

**Scenario:** You need to schedule an important team meeting to discuss project updates. Write a formal email to your team.

---

> [!example] Model Answer
>
> **To:** team@company.com
> **Subject:** Team Meeting – [Topic] on [Date] at [Time]
>
> Dear Team,
>
> I would like to inform you that a team meeting has been scheduled to discuss **[meeting agenda – e.g., Q3 project progress and upcoming milestones]**.
>
> **Meeting Details:**
> - 📅 **Date:** [Day, Date Month Year]
> - ⏰ **Time:** [Time] (IST)
> - 📍 **Venue / Platform:** [Conference Room / MS Teams / Zoom Link]
>
> Kindly ensure your attendance and come prepared with updates on your respective tasks. Please confirm your availability by **[RSVP date]**.
>
> Should you have any agenda points to add, please share them with me by **[date]** so that they can be incorporated.
>
> Looking forward to a productive session.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]
> [Designation]

### Q6 – Write an email to a vendor requesting a price quotation

**Scenario:** Your company requires a new software tool. Write an email to a vendor requesting a quotation.

---

> [!example] Model Answer
>
> **To:** sales@vendorcompany.com
> **Subject:** Request for Quotation – [Product/Service Name]
>
> Dear Sir/Madam,
>
> I am **[Your Name]**, [Designation] at **[Your Company Name]**. We are currently evaluating solutions for **[brief need – e.g., project management software / cloud storage services]** for our organization.
>
> We would like to request a detailed price quotation for the following:
>
> 1. [Product/Service 1]
> 2. [Product/Service 2]
> 3. [Any specific feature or customization]
>
> Could you also include details regarding licensing terms, support services, and implementation timelines?
>
> Please send the quotation to this email address at your earliest convenience. We would appreciate a response by **[deadline date]**.
>
> Thank you for your time. We look forward to hearing from you.
>
> Regards,
> [Your Name]
> [Designation] | [Company Name]
> [Phone Number]

### Q7 – Write an email to your manager acknowledging receipt of feedback

**Scenario:** Your manager shared performance feedback with you. Write an email acknowledging it and expressing your commitment to improvement.

---

> [!example] Model Answer
>
> **To:** manager@company.com
> **Subject:** Acknowledgement of Performance Feedback
>
> Dear Mr./Ms. [Last Name],
>
> Thank you for taking the time to share your valuable feedback regarding my performance. I truly appreciate your insights and the effort you put into guiding my professional development.
>
> I have carefully reviewed the feedback and I understand the areas where improvement is expected. I am committed to working on **[specific areas mentioned]** and will take the necessary steps to enhance my performance going forward.
>
> I would also welcome the opportunity to discuss this further with you to gain a clearer understanding of your expectations. Please let me know a suitable time for a brief meeting.
>
> Once again, thank you for your continued support and mentorship.
>
> Regards,
> [Your Name]
> [Designation] | [Department]

### Q8 – Write an email to inform your team about a policy change

**Scenario:** The company has introduced a new attendance policy. Communicate this change to your team formally.

---

> [!example] Model Answer
>
> **To:** team@company.com
> **Subject:** Important Update – Revised Attendance Policy Effective [Date]
>
> Dear Team,
>
> I would like to bring to your attention an important update regarding our company's **Attendance Policy**, effective from **[date]**.
>
> As per the revised policy:
>
> 1. All employees are required to log in by **[time]** on working days.
> 2. Any absence must be reported to the reporting manager at least **[X hours]** in advance.
> 3. Work-from-home requests must be submitted through the HR portal a minimum of **[X days]** prior.
>
> Please ensure you familiarise yourself with the complete policy document, which is attached for your reference. Adherence to this policy is mandatory for all team members.
>
> Should you have any questions or clarifications, feel free to reach out to me or the HR department directly.
>
> Thank you for your cooperation.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]
> [Designation]

## 🗂️ Email Format Cheat Sheet

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **To** | Recipient's official email |
| **Subject** | Specific, concise (5–10 words) |
| **Salutation** | Dear Mr./Ms. [Last Name] / Dear Team |
| **Opening Line** | Polite opening (hope you're well…) |
| **Body** | Purpose → Details → Request/Action |
| **Closing** | Thank you / Looking forward |
| **Sign-off** | Regards / Sincerely / Yours faithfully |
| **Signature** | Name, Designation, Department, Contact |
