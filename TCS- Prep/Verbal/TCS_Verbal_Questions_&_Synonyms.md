## 📖 SECTION 2 — Reading Comprehension (RC) Practice

> [!tip] Strategy
> - Read the **last line of each paragraph** first to get the gist
> - Eliminate **extreme answer choices** (always, never, completely)
> - Inference questions → stay close to what is **implied**, not assumed

---

### 📄 RC Passage 1 – Technology & Society

**Passage:**
The rapid proliferation of technology in everyday life has ushered in unprecedented changes in the way humans communicate, work, and socialize. While digital tools have dramatically increased efficiency and connectivity, critics argue that excessive dependence on technology has eroded interpersonal skills and diminished attention spans. Studies indicate that heavy smartphone usage correlates with reduced face-to-face interactions, leading to a paradox wherein individuals are digitally connected yet socially isolated. The challenge for modern society lies in striking a balance — leveraging technological advancements without sacrificing human values and relationships.

---

**Q1. What is the central theme of the passage?**
- (A) Technology is purely harmful to society
- (B) Technology improves human productivity alone
- (C) Technology brings both benefits and social challenges
- (D) Smartphones cause mental illness

> [!success] Answer: (C)
> The passage presents both the benefits of technology (efficiency, connectivity) and its downsides (eroded interpersonal skills), making (C) the balanced and correct answer.

---

**Q2. What does the word "proliferation" most nearly mean in this context?**
- (A) Decline
- (B) Rapid spread
- (C) Invention
- (D) Regulation

> [!success] Answer: (B)
> "Proliferation" means rapid increase or spread, which fits the context of technology expanding into everyday life.

---

**Q3. According to the passage, what is the "paradox" referred to?**
- (A) Technology reduces productivity
- (B) People are digitally connected but socially isolated
- (C) Smartphones increase attention spans
- (D) Critics support technology adoption

> [!success] Answer: (B)
> The passage explicitly states: "individuals are digitally connected yet socially isolated" — this is the paradox.

---

**Q4. What does the author suggest as a solution?**
- (A) Stop using technology entirely
- (B) Use only traditional communication
- (C) Balance technology use with human values
- (D) Replace social interaction with digital tools

> [!success] Answer: (C)
> The author says "striking a balance — leveraging technological advancements without sacrificing human values."

### 📄 RC Passage 2 – Environment

**Passage:**
Climate change has emerged as one of the most pressing concerns of the 21st century. The increasing concentration of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere, primarily due to industrial activities and deforestation, has led to a measurable rise in global temperatures. Scientists warn that if current trends persist, the consequences will be catastrophic — ranging from rising sea levels to extreme weather events and biodiversity loss. Governments worldwide have pledged to reduce carbon emissions, but implementation remains inconsistent. Individual action, though necessary, is insufficient without systemic change at the policy level.

---

**Q1. What is the primary cause of climate change according to the passage?**
- (A) Natural disasters
- (B) Industrial activities and deforestation
- (C) Rising sea levels
- (D) Biodiversity loss

> [!success] Answer: (B)
> The passage directly attributes climate change to "industrial activities and deforestation."

---

**Q2. The word "systemic" in the last sentence means:**
- (A) Medical

- (C) Relating to the whole system
- (D) Temporary

> [!success] Answer: (C)
> Systemic = relating to a whole system, here referring to policy-level changes.

---

**Q3. What can be inferred about individual action from the passage?**

## 🔍 SECTION 4 — Error Identification

> Identify the part of the sentence that contains a grammatical error.

---

**Q1.** (A) Neither the manager / (B) nor his assistants / (C) were present / (D) at the meeting yesterday.

> [!success] Answer: (C) — "were" should be "was"
> When "neither...nor" is used and the subject closer to the verb is singular ("his assistants" = plural here, so "were" is actually correct). ⚠️ Trick: Closest noun to verb = "assistants" (plural) → "were" is correct. **No error** — answer is (D).

---

**Q2.** (A) She is one of / (B) those students who / (C) has scored / (D) full marks in the exam.

> [!success] Answer: (C) — "has" should be "have"
> "One of those students who" → the relative clause refers to "students" (plural), so use "have."

---

**Q3.** (A) The committee has / (B) decided to postpone / (C) the meeting for / (D) next Friday.

> [!success] Answer: (C) — "for" should be "to"
> Postpone something **to** a future time, not **for**.

---

**Q4.** (A) Each of the boys / (B) in the class / (C) were given / (D) a separate assignment.

> [!success] Answer: (C) — "were" should be "was"
> "Each" is singular → takes a singular verb "was."

---

**Q5.** (A) He denied / (B) to have taken / (C) the money / (D) from the drawer.

> [!success] Answer: (B) — "to have taken" should be "having taken"
> "Deny" takes a gerund (-ing form), not an infinitive.

## 🔀 SECTION 5 — Para Jumbles

> Arrange the sentences into a logical paragraph.

---

**Q1.** Arrange sentences P, Q, R, S to form a coherent paragraph.

- **P:** This has led to a surge in demand for sustainable alternatives.
- **Q:** Plastic pollution has become a major environmental crisis worldwide.
- **R:** Governments are now implementing bans on single-use plastics.
- **S:** Millions of tonnes of plastic end up in oceans every year, harming marine life.

> [!success] Answer: Q → S → P → R
> Introduce the problem (Q) → Give evidence (S) → Effect of evidence (P) → Solution (R)

---

**Q2.** Arrange sentences A, B, C, D:

- **A:** Regular exercise also helps maintain a healthy weight and reduces disease risk.
- **B:** It is essential to dedicate at least 30 minutes a day to physical activity.
- **C:** The benefits of exercise on mental health are well-documented.
- **D:** Exercise releases endorphins, which are known to reduce stress and anxiety.

> [!success] Answer: C → D → A → B
> Topic sentence (C) → Support with evidence (D) → Additional benefit (A) → Recommendation (B)
